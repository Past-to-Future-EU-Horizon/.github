# Past to Future

Past to Future (P2F) is a 4-year Horizon Europe research project which launched in March 2025 and brings together 25 partners from a wide-range of disciplines in future climate model development, paleo-climate data collection, and applied mathematics. Our goal is to make optimal use of past climatic information to better understand Earth’s climate response to various kinds of forcing, focusing on abrupt climate transitions and tipping points.

You can find out more on our website, [past2future.org](https://past2future.org/)

This GitHub Organization hosts the code, libraries, and containers we're building for the Past 2 Future Portal, found at [portal.past2future.org](https://portal.past2future.org/) and [api.past2future.org](https://api.past2future.org/)
